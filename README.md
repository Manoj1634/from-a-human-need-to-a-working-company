# From a Human Need to a Working Company

*An engineer's notes on what sits above engineering. By Manoj.*

AI is writing more of our code. So where does the hard part go now?

I'm an AI engineer. I build systems inside companies, but I've never built a company myself. As AI took over more of my coding, I got curious about everything around the code: which problems are worth solving, why people pay for some solutions and not others, and what turns a solution into a working company.

This series is what I've read, observed and pieced together along the way. It isn't a playbook. It's a set of notes you can use to reflect on your own work.

## Who might find this useful

- **Engineers** watching AI write more of their code and wondering where their value moves next
- **People who want to build a company** and want to know what it takes beyond a good product
- **Consultants and forward-deployed engineers** who want to see where their clients' markets are heading
