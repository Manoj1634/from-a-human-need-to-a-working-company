# The Series

*From a Human Need to a Working Company, in eight short parts. By Manoj Padala.*

Each part takes about 4 minutes and ends with a link to the next.

1. **[Coding Is Becoming a Solved Problem. What Comes Next?](01-coding-is-becoming-solved.md)**: Why AI writing more code moves the hard part somewhere else, and who this series is for.
2. **[What Does It Take to Build a Company?](02-what-does-it-take-to-build-a-company.md)**: What companies have that one person doesn't, and what engineers already bring.
3. **[Every Business Ends at a Human Need](03-every-business-ends-at-a-human-need.md)**: Human needs don't change; products are just new ways to meet them.
4. **[Value Is What the Customer Perceives](04-value-is-what-the-customer-perceives.md)**: Why value is what the customer believes, not how hard the work was.
5. **[One Person, Every Seat: Why a Company Works Better Together](05-one-person-every-seat.md)**: The seats in a company, and why a team does more than one person alone.
6. **["Done" Is a Version: How Engineers Already Think Like a Business](06-done-is-a-version.md)**: How the way engineers ship in versions is already how businesses grow.
7. **[Getting It to People: Aim, Story, Funding and Selling](07-getting-it-to-people.md)**: Where to aim, how to tell the story, and how funding and selling fit in.
8. **[From a Human Need to a Working Company](08-from-a-human-need-to-a-working-company.md)**: Everything put together as one path, plus the principles I'm taking with me.
